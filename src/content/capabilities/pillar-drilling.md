Many turned components need a hole to work - a split-pin hole in a clevis pin, a pivot hole in a lever, or mounting holes in a bracket. Our **HMT pillar drilling machine** handles these secondary drilling operations in-house.

## Typical drilling work

- **Cross-holes** in pins and rods for split pins and retaining clips
- **Through-holes and blind holes** in brackets, pads and levers
- **Pilot holes** that prepare a part for later operations

## One workshop, complete components

Combining stock cutting, CNC turning, polygon milling, drilling and centreless grinding means many components can be completed under one roof in Padi, Chennai.
