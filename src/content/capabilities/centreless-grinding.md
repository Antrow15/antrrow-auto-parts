Many pins, rods and shafts need an outside diameter that is more consistent and smoother than turning alone provides. At Antrrow Auto Parts, our **Bhavans Sons centreless grinder** gives turned and cylindrical components a ground external finish.

## Why centreless grinding

Centreless grinding supports the part on a work rest between a grinding wheel and a regulating wheel. Without centre holes or chucking, it suits long runs of straight cylindrical parts such as:

- Clutch pins and dowel-style pins
- Weight-balancing rods and guide rods
- Piston-rod blanks
- Rollers and cylindrical spacers

## Grinding as part of a complete route

Most jobs follow a simple route: **cut stock, CNC turn, centreless grind, inspect and pack**. Keeping grinding in-house means one point of contact and fewer handovers for your component.
