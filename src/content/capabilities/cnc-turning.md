CNC turning is the core of our work at Antrrow Auto Parts. On our **ACE CNC lathe**, we turn bar and rod stock into precise cylindrical components - pins, bushes, sleeves, spacers, shafts and special turned parts - for automotive and industrial customers across Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

## How we approach a CNC turning job

1. **Drawing review** - we check the drawing revision, material, critical dimensions, finish and quantity.
2. **Stock preparation** - bars are cut to blank length on our stock cutting machine.
3. **Turning** - outside diameters, faces, steps, shoulders, chamfers and grooves are turned to the approved program.
4. **Secondary operations** - where required, parts move to polygon milling, drilling or centreless grinding.
5. **Inspection and packing** - dimensions on the drawing are checked before parts are packed and dispatched.

## Typical CNC turned components

Our CNC turned components are used in clutch and driveline assemblies, linkages, fixtures and general machinery. Common examples include clutch pins, stepped pins, shoulder pins, bushes, distance sleeves, guide rods and threaded studs.
