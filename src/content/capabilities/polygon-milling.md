Antrrow Auto Parts is known locally as a **CNC turned components, grinding and polygon milling works**. Our **Trishul polygon milling machine** adds flats and polygonal profiles to turned pins, studs and shafts - quickly and consistently across a batch.

## Where polygon milling is used

- **Wrench flats** so a shaft or stud can be held or tightened with a spanner.
- **Drive flats** to transmit torque between mating parts.
- **Anti-rotation flats** on locating pins, pivot pins and clutch pins.
- **Hexagonal or square ends** on special studs and turned bolts.

## Turning and polygon milling under one roof

Because CNC turning and polygon milling are done in the same Padi, Chennai workshop, components move between operations without outside handling. That helps keep lead times practical and batch identification simple.
