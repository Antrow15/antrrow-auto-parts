Every turned component starts as a bar. Our **Laxon cutting machine** prepares bar, rod and tube stock into blanks of repeatable length, so that machining starts from consistent material.

## Part of the machining route

Stock cutting is the first shop-floor step for most of our jobs:

1. Raw material is received and checked against the job.
2. Bars are cut into batch-identified blanks.
3. Blanks move to CNC turning, polygon milling, drilling and centreless grinding as the drawing requires.

## Bundle cutting for batches

For repeat jobs, suitable bars can be clamped and cut together, which reduces preparation time on pins, shafts, spacers and other high-count turned parts.
