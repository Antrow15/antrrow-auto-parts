Not every part fits a catalogue. Send us your **drawing or sample**, and we will review whether it suits our machines, define a **process route**, and quote for samples, prototypes or repeat batches.

## What to send

- Drawing with revision, or a physical sample
- Material and grade
- Quantity - first order and expected repeat volume
- Finish and any special requirements
- Target delivery date
