Alongside turned components, we supply **clutch pads, support brackets and mounting brackets** to clutch and driveline component makers. These parts are customer-specific, so each one starts with a review of the drawing and the manufacturing route.

## How we work on pads and brackets

1. Review the drawing, material and quantity.
2. Agree the route - including machining and drilling of holes and features.
3. Inspect dimensions on the drawing, then pack and dispatch.

Share your drawing and we will confirm the route and quotation.
