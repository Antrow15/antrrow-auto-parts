From short stepped shafts to straight guide rods, we make **rods and shafts** that are cut to length, CNC turned and, where needed, **centreless ground** for a consistent outside diameter.

## Typical work

- **Weight-balancing rods** for industrial equipment
- **Guide rods** and straight rods
- **Stepped shafts** with shoulders, grooves and chamfers
- **Short shafts** with flats or cross-holes
