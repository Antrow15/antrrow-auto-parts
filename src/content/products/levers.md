Clutch levers transfer pedal effort into release movement, and they depend on accurate pivots. We supply **3-finger and 4-finger levers, lever pivots and linkage levers** to clutch and driveline component makers, together with the **pivot pins and bushes** that go with them.

## Why source levers and pins together

When levers, pivot pins and bushes come from one supplier, fit-related questions are easier to resolve and batch planning is simpler.
