Pins are small parts with an important job: locating, pivoting, linking and transmitting load. At Antrrow Auto Parts, we make **clutch pins, dowel-style pins, stepped and shoulder pins, pivot pins and clevis-style pins** to customer drawings in our Padi, Chennai workshop.

## Made the way your drawing needs

- **CNC turned** diameters, steps, shoulders, chamfers and grooves
- **Polygon milled** flats for anti-rotation or wrench features
- **Drilled** cross-holes for split pins and clevis applications
- **Centreless ground** outside diameters where the drawing calls for it

## Used in

Clutch and driveline assemblies, linkages and levers, fixtures and general industrial machinery.
